## ReSTIR GI

* [Intro to ReSTIR](https://intro-to-restir.cwyman.org/) — a general overview, a good starting point.
* [Understanding the Math Behind ReSTIR GI](https://agraphicsguynotes.com/posts/understanding_the_math_behind_restir_gi/)
* [Reservoir Sampling](https://samwho.dev/reservoir-sampling/) — the core technique ReSTIR is built on, explained interactively.

> If you are new to ReSTIR, read the reservoir sampling article first. The rest makes a lot more sense once you understand how a reservoir picks a sample from a stream.
